Boardz · new card directions · punk meets cyberspace

Two new Boardz cards

Same parts, new layouts. Photos stay in full color. Type is Barlow, with the design system's neon tape, tags, mono kicker, rainbow seam and 8% bloom. Every card keeps the post type up front, the seeking dashes on posts looking for something, the network with its wire companion, the cropped hero sticker, the Giftz logo and the heart. Each card has two versions with the hero placed a little differently; a post draws A or B from its id. Tap a card to open it: it grows into the open card through a glitch. Tap the X to close. The heart saves.

1 · Wheatpaste · posts with a photo

A street poster pasted over other posters. The user photo runs full bleed in color under a halftone, the Barlow headline sits on torn strips, the post type on neon tape. Opening peels it into a full-height poster with the details on a pasted paper strip.
