# PDX Pride Guide — Brand Identity

*Portland's queer events, all year.*

This is the source of truth for how PDX Pride Guide looks, sounds, and behaves.
If you're writing copy, designing a screen, or building a feature, it should feel
like it came from here.

---

## 1. Who we are

**PDX Pride Guide is the year-round home for Portland's LGBTQ+ nightlife, events,
and community.** It started as a Pride-Week guide and grew into a living map of
queer Portland — every party, class, market, and drag show; the bars and venues
that host us; and the community boards where the scene looks out for each other.

**One-liner:** Your welcoming map to queer Portland — find the party, back the
places that host it, and take care of each other.

**Elevator version:** A community-built, year-round guide to Portland's LGBTQ+
events and queer-owned spaces, with mutual-aid boards baked in. Not a corporate
listings site — a neighborhood one.

---

## 2. What we believe (values)

1. **Community over clout.** We exist to connect people and keep the scene alive,
   not to farm engagement. Every feature should answer "does this help someone
   find their people or support the community?"
2. **Mutual aid is the point.** Missed Connections, Gifting, and the Gig Board
   aren't add-ons — they're the heart. Keep it free, keep it kind, keep it moving.
3. **Spend queer, keep us healthy.** We send money and attention toward
   queer-owned and genuinely queer-friendly venues, promoters, and workers.
4. **Everybody's invited — and protected.** Trans, nonbinary, two-spirit, kink,
   sex-positive, sex-worker-friendly, sober, and everyone in between. We build
   for the whole spectrum, and we age-gate and label sensitive spaces with respect,
   never shame.
5. **Portland-rooted.** This is a local project with local taste — neighborhoods,
   real venues, real people. Not a franchise.
6. **Made by us, for us.** Community-built, a little scrappy, transparent about it.
   "Pardon our dust" beats fake polish.

---

## 3. Personality & voice

**We sound like a well-connected friend who runs the group chat** — warm, funny,
a little irreverent, but genuinely looking out for you. Confident, never corporate.
Celebratory, never saccharine. Direct, never cold.

**Voice sliders:**
- Warm 90 / Cool 10
- Playful 70 / Serious 30
- Plainspoken 85 / Polished 15
- Proud & loud 80 / Understated 20

**Do sound like:**
- "Show up for each other."
- "Every day is Pride."
- "Spend queer, keep us healthy."
- "Stay kind · stay anonymous · reveal when ready."
- "Keeping the lights on isn't free — buy me a coffee or a muffin, no pressure."
- "We're rebuilding this into a year-round home for Portland's LGBTQ+ events."
- "Made with 🖤 in Portland."

**Don't sound like:**
- "Leverage our platform to maximize your event discovery experience."
- "Celebrate diversity and inclusion!" (hollow, corporate-Pride).
- Anything that talks *about* the community instead of *to* it.
- Scolding or clinical language around sex, kink, or 18+/21+ spaces.

**Tone by moment:**
- **Marketing / heroes:** proud, punchy, short. Uppercase display is fine.
- **Helpful / product UI:** plain and clear. Say what the button does.
- **Sensitive (age gates, moderation, safety):** calm, matter-of-fact, respectful.
  Inform and protect; don't lecture.
- **Errors:** honest and human. "Couldn't reach that — try again" over "Error 500."

## 5. Visual identity

The look is **neon signage on a dark room** — think a queer club at night: black
walls, glass, and glowing color. High contrast, confident, a little maximalist,
always legible.

### Color

**Foundation**
- Ink Black `#0B0B0B` (primary background), deeper `#050506`, warm charcoal `#141414`
- Card edge `#24242C`, hairline `#1E1E26`
- Text white `#FFFFFF`, muted `#C9C9D2`, faint `#9A9AA2`

**Neons (the pride palette)** — used for accents, glows, and gradients, not walls of it:
- Neon Lime / Yellow `#CCFF00` (a.k.a. `#FFEE00` in gradients) — primary "go" accent
- Neon Cyan `#00FFFF`
- Neon Magenta / Pink `#FF00CC`
- Neon Orange `#FF6600`
- Neon Purple `#6E3DFF`

**The rainbow seam** — our signature flourish, a flowing top-edge gradient:
`linear-gradient(90deg, #00FFFF, #FFEE00, #FF00CC, #FF6600, #00FFFF)`, animated
to drift. Use it sparingly as a "spine" on hero cards, modals, and section tops.

**Rules of thumb:** dark first, neon as punctuation. One or two accents per surface,
not five. Gradients for celebration; solid neon for a single primary action. Keep
text on solid backgrounds — never neon text on neon.

### Type

- **Display / headings:** Barlow, heavy (800–900), often UPPERCASE, tight leading.
  Loud and structural — this is the "neon sign."
- **Body:** Barlow, regular/medium. Clean and readable.
- **Kickers / labels / meta:** monospace, small, wide letter-spacing, uppercase
  (e.g. `UNDER CONSTRUCTION`, `THE DIRECTORY FOR US`). This is a signature texture.

### Surfaces & motifs

- **Glass cards** (`pdx-glass-card`) — dark, subtly translucent, thin edge, soft
  corner sheen; optional colored left-accent bar for category/emphasis.
- **Glow** — soft neon bloom behind primary actions and avatars (the "pride ring").
- **The rainbow seam** (above) as the recurring signature.
- **Avatars with pride rings** — members express identity with illustrated avatars
  and glowing rings; encourage it, never require it.
- **Category colors** — bars, food, cafes, venues, shops each carry a consistent hue.

### Imagery

- Real flyers, real venues, real people. Community photography over stock.
- Never straight-wash or de-queer imagery to look "safe."
- 18+/21+ and sex-positive content lives in labeled, age-gated spaces — present it
  confidently and without apology, but keep it out of the general front door.

### Logo direction

Wordmark-forward, neon-sign energy — heavy Barlow with the rainbow seam or a glow.
Works on black first. Keep a monochrome white version for tight spots. Favicon /
app icon leans on the glow + a single bold mark.

## 6. Writing mechanics

- Sentence case for most UI; UPPERCASE reserved for display headings and mono kickers.
- Short lines. Active voice. Cut throat-clearing.
- Oxford-comma optional but be consistent within a screen.
- Emoji: sparingly and with intent — 🖤 🌈 ✦ 🚧 as accents, not decoration soup.
- Say "queer" and "LGBTQ+" naturally; "LGBTQ2SIA+" in formal/nonprofit contexts.
- People-first, community-second, product-last. Talk *to* people, not *about* a "userbase."
- Money asks are light and honest ("coffee or a muffin, no pressure"), never guilt-trippy.

---

## 7. Inclusivity & safety principles

- **Default to they/them** when pronouns are unknown; never guess from a name.
- **Respect anonymity** — Missed Connections and boards let people stay anonymous
  and reveal when ready.
- **Label, don't shame.** Sex-positive, kink, and 18+/21+ spaces are flagged and
  age-gated so people opt in — framed as care, not censorship.
- **Back the workers.** Sex-worker-friendly, gig-worker-friendly; the Gig Board and
  directory treat everyone's labor as real.
- **Accessibility is table stakes** — legible contrast, tap targets, reduced-motion
  respect, works on the phone people actually carry.
