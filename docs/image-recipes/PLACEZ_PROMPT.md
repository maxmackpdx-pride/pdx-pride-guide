## Label the supplied images

- Target sources: multiple distinct real photographs of the requested venue. Architecture, facade, windows, signage, and existing-ground evidence come from these real source images only.
- Step 5 style endpoint: the appropriate Holocene/Alberta Rose or Stag/Eagle artwork. Transfer treatment and composition balance only; do not transfer the depicted venue.
- Step 4 physical references: both supplied black torn-paper collage images. Transfer paper, print, tape, staple, and wear material qualities, not their scene objects or slogans.
- Rainbow color reference: the bundled approved Sports Bra rainbow in approved-rainbow-road.md (see PLACEZ_BUILDING_RECIPE.md), plus the actual Zaylist primary logo when available. Transfer its colors/order only, never its text, logo, objects, or ground geometry.
- Current target, when revising: the image to correct. Preserve its approved composition except for the user's requested changes.

## Prompt scaffold

“Create a standalone landscape PlaceZ building artwork for [PLACE] using multiple actual reference photographs together for its architecture. Accurately reconstruct the source's visible [LANDMARKS], including [WINDOW GROUPS/PANES/CONTENTS], [DOORS], [ROOFLINE], and [PHOTOGRAPHICALLY SUPPORTED SIGNAGE: WORDING/DESIGN/SIZE/PLACEMENT]. Apply the specified styling and colors while reconstructing the storefront from the multiple angles. Reasonable imagination is allowed for perspective, ground/road extent below, and atmospheric sky above. This latitude does not permit invented objects, signs, text, or anything in the windows. Do not add, relocate, or redesign signs, lettering, logos, window contents, fixtures, new architectural features, or other concrete scene objects unsupported by the target photographs. Do not create a sign from the venue name or listing text. Preserve unreadable signs as unreadable rather than guessing their words or adding pseudo-text. Make a strictly face-forward, eye-level, photographic HDR treatment, not an oblique corner view with believable materials, controlled verticals, rich tonal detail, and the approved wide framing. Leave a substantial visible area of atmospheric sky above the roof behind the wireframes; no black mesh-only replacement of the sky. No cars or vehicles. Resolve formerly occluded features from the other photographs. Reasonably reconstruct continuous surfaces where needed, but do not invent hidden objects, signs, text, new openings, or window contents.

“Use the endpoint image only as a style reference. Add **3D wireframing**: fine, tight, blueprint-like spatial planes, grids, triangulated meshes, and perspective construction lines around the actual building silhouette and densely through the sky and surrounding negative space, with substantially less on the facade. ## Iteration order

1. Determine the place type and assigned/intended card color, then the complementary accent. This is Tucker's corrected Step 1.
2. Resolve the exact venue/address and requested period. Find and inspect multiple distinct authentic source photographs using the sourcing workflow in PLACEZ_BUILDING_RECIPE.md. Select each for the missing architectural evidence it supplies, not search rank or a three-photo quota. Record complementary coverage, unresolved gaps, provenance, dates, rights, visible window counts/positions/panes/contents, and sign wording/design/size/placement where supported.
3. Establish the face-forward photographic HDR facade, visible atmospheric sky, and car-free road/ground below. Reasonable perspective, ground-extent, and sky reconstruction is allowed. Verify all concrete objects, architectural features, signs, text, and window contents against the photos; distinguish composition estimates from photographic facts. Correct unsupported signage or other additions before styling; obtain a clearer view or retain and disclose uncertainty instead of guessing.
4. Develop tight blueprint-like **3D wireframing** through the sky and around the building edges, using 70% primary category accent and 30% complementary accent, with no added neon tubes. Match the approved Sports Bra continuous logo-spectrum overlay and its texture/strength on blacktop only; do not introduce a road solely for the effect and omit it when blacktop is absent unless the current image has explicit permission for another existing surface.
5. Add black Xerox overprint, then tactile collage from both historical Step 4 references. Make the white torn-paper edges and fibers match the verified primary category accent.
6. Restore/increase photorealism and physical print/paper wear without changing architecture. Recheck windows, all signage, other real-world details, and sky. Reject invented objects, signage, text, window contents, or new architectural features introduced by styling, while allowing the stated compositional reconstruction.
7. Deliver the artwork together with the real reference images on the first delivery, with credits and any remaining fidelity uncertainty.

A single combined pass may be useful for a new venue, but it does not waive the separate checks. Correct only the failing aspect on subsequent passes and supply the real source again. Judge the output against the original photo and bundled endpoint, not just against its preceding generated version.
