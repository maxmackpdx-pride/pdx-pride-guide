06 — What we believe

Six values

01
Community over clout. Help someone find their people or support the scene — not farm engagement.
02
Mutual aid is the point. Missed Connections, Gifting, the Gig Board — the heart, not add-ons. Free, kind, moving.
03
Spend queer, keep us healthy. Money and attention toward queer-owned and genuinely queer-friendly places.
04
Everybody's invited — and protected. The whole spectrum. Age-gate and label sensitive spaces with respect, never shame.
05
Portland-rooted. Local taste, real neighborhoods, real venues. Not a franchise.
06
Made by us, for us. Community-built, a little scrappy, transparent. "Pardon our dust" beats fake polish.
07 — Inclusivity & safety

Label, don't shame

›
Default to they/them when pronouns are unknown; never guess from a name.
›
Respect anonymity — boards let people stay anonymous and reveal when ready.
›
Age-gate as care — sex-positive, kink, and 18+/21+ spaces are flagged so people opt in. Framed as care, not censorship.
›
Back the workers — sex-worker- and gig-worker-friendly; everyone's labor is real.
›
Accessibility is table stakes — contrast, tap targets, reduced-motion, works on the phone people carry.
