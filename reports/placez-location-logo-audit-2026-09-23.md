## Artwork

Eleven generic PNG wordmarks were replaced with organization-published assets for Gaylabration, Portland Queer Arts Foundation, Multnomah County Cultural Coalition, Oregon Arts Commission, Regional Arts & Culture Council, Fertile Ground Festival, Portland Area Theatre Alliance, Radical Faerie Arts Fest, IL Youth 2 Youth, Queer Social Club, and MakeWithPDX. The remaining existing assets were retained where a branded artwork already existed or a suitable official asset could not be verified. Open Space Dance's official square logo is vertically arranged and unreadable at card size, so its legible existing wordmark was retained. The logo cache version was bumped.
