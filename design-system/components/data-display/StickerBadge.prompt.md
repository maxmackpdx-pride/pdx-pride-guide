**StickerBadge**, the collage slogan sticker. Chunky rotated display type on a hard-shadow neon chip. The loudest, most "zine" brand motif.

```jsx
<StickerBadge color="lime" rotate={-4}>Keep Portland Weird</StickerBadge>
<StickerBadge color="pink" rotate={3} size="lg">Yas Gurl</StickerBadge>
<StickerBadge color="rainbow" rotate={-2}>Made by the Scene</StickerBadge>
```

- **color**: `lime` / `pink` / `cyan` / `purple` / `yellow` / `rainbow` / `paper` / `outline`.
- **size**: `sm` / `md` / `lg`. **rotate**: degrees, vary per instance for collage chaos.
- **Placement**: in hero and promo blocks, stickers sit directly ABOVE or BELOW the title (like a kicker or a stamp on the headline), not floating in dead space. One to three per block, at varied rotations.
- Use for slogans and promo flair only, NOT functional labels (use Badge for those).
