## Logo Scale

**30%**

A 30% application of Swiss design principles creates a cleaner, more functional and timeless logo while keeping the core identity intact.

## 1. Typography

**Aa**  
*Neue Haas Grotesk Bold*

Clean, neutral and highly legible. Supports clarity and functionality.

## 2. Color

**Vibrant Rainbow Palette** (maintained intentionally for brand recognition and visual impact):

- Red
- Orange
- Yellow
- Green
- Blue
- Purple

*Use the full gradient for the primary wordmark. Solid swatches for accents and supporting elements.*

## 3. Layout

Centered composition with balanced proportions and clear spacing.

## 4. Space

Generous negative space enhances visibility and impact.

## 5. Simplicity

Removed unnecessary effects. Focus on essential elements for a timeless and scalable mark.

## 6. Consistency

Consistent design across platforms and applications ensures brand integrity.

---

**Design Principles Applied:** Swiss-inspired minimalism for scalability and timelessness. The 30% scale reference ensures the logo remains impactful even at smaller sizes without losing legibility or core identity.

**Integration Notes:** Aligns with Zaylist neon/rainbow motifs and glassmorphism tokens while providing a clean, professional wordmark option for headers, merch, or partner co-branding.
