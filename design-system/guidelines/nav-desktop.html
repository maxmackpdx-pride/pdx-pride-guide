Specification

Primary nav

Home, About, Events, Places, Nude Beaches, a Boards dropdown, Promoters. Order and labels come from PRIMARY_NAV. Schedule is not here: it lives in the mobile Events sheet and the Events page.

Link states

Idle #aaa, hover white, active acid yellow with a 3px cyan-to-yellow underbar and an 8px cyan glow. Display font 700 at --site-nav-size, .06em, uppercase, never wrapping.

Dropdown

Chevron rotates 180 degrees when open. The panel is the tab-sheet surface: rgba(8,8,10,.96), a 1px black edge with a black ring and lift shadow, 18px radius, a display-font heading, and rows on 2% white inside 10px radius hairlines that take the accent on hover. Identical to the mobile Events and Boards sheets, so one component reads the same on both sizes.

Right cluster

Hub sits left of a 1px #333 seam with equal margins, then the avatar and its caret. From 1024px up the cluster is pushed right with margin-left:auto.

Unread dot

8px magenta dot on the Hub link with a 1.5px page-colored ring, breathing out to a 6px transparent halo on a 2.2s loop.

Join button

Signed out, the cluster is replaced by the solid Button: lit acid fill, black keyline, top bevel, floor shade and an acid bloom. Identical atoms to .pdxBtn--solid, so it can never drift from 09.1. The flat magenta offset it used to carry is retired. This is one of the few places the brutal shadow is still correct.

Sizing

The header scales from the logo: --site-logo-h: clamp(52px,7.5vw,72px) drives nav size, padding and gaps, so the row never breaks between 1024 and 1320px.

Seam

The 2.7px animated seam is a real element under the header, not a pseudo, so Seam Charge can drive it while the router navigates or a query fetches.

Never

Nav chrome is locked unless it is explicitly requested: black outlines, cyan for active and handles only. No touch-target changes, no reordering, no extra links without an ask.
