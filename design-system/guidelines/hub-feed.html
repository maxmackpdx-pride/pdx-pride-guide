Hub & news feed

The signed-in home. A three-column shell with a sectioned rail on the left, the scene feed in the middle and two live rails on the right. It is the one place on the site that behaves like a feed: everything else is a directory or a board.

Ported from client/src/components/hub/HubV2.tsx · HubV2Shell.tsx · sections/HubFeed.tsx · sections/HubFeedCard.tsx · hub/types.ts · shared/hubFeed.ts · inbox/panel/sheet.ts

Rail accents

Cyan, lime, green and pink inset rails, one per nav item, set as --_c. The unread count rides the item as a pink chip.

Right rail

Your next moves lists up to three upcoming nights you are hosting or attending, day-dotted and sorted by start time, with an empty state that tells you how to fill it. Who to follow shows three discover suggestions, and collapses to a single explanatory line once you follow everyone.

Above the feed

The weather forecast strip sits above the feed on the Feed and Post sections only. A PWA install banner can appear above everything.

Profile stats

Followers, Following, Events (hosting plus going), Posts. Missing values render as a dash, never a zero.

04 · Card anatomy

Author

Avatar, display name, then a badge and an action line. A venue logo renders contained with padding rather than cropped, and deep-links to its directory place.

Posted by

When an event or venue posts under a shared identity, the real person appears as a "posted by" sub-line so co-hosts stay attributable.

Event embed

Title, venue, day, start, admission and going count, with a day-colored left edge. Several events from one poster inside a short window bundle into a single card with a row each.

Glow

Board cards (gig, gifting, hub) take a 1px accent border and 0 0 22px -9px of their board color. Everything else stays on the plain hairline.

Engagement

Like and reply counts sit in the footer. Gig and hub posts thread inline; spotted and gifting replies stay private and show a count only.

Follow state

Cards carry the viewer's follow state so Unfollow is available in place. Soft launch follows everyone by default.

Pinned

Pinned scene cards sit below live activity and new posts stack above them, so the feed never opens on something static.

Never

No infinite decorative motion in the feed, no day color on a badge (badges are kind, days belong to events), and no navigation to read a post: board cards open an overlay in place.
