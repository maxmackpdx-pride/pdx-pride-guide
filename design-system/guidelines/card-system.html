Card system
Deep glass

One card shell drives Events, the Directory, the ads and every community board. This page is the shell: its anatomy, its one accent rule, and how each surface re-skins it. The colour values and page layouts it references live in other panels, so this one points at them instead of repeating them.

Every card on the site is the same object: a near-black OLED slab with a neon edge, a glass sheen, a rainbow refract seam and a soft outer bloom. There is exactly one shell. A surface does not get its own card, it gets the shell pointed at its own accent. Pick the shell, set one variable, and the surface identity falls out of it.

Source of truth: tokens/glass.css (the token layer) · docs/handoffs/deep-glass-2026-07-16/Card System.html (the visual spec) · docs/LIVE_DESIGN_STANDARD.md (live product is truth)

01 · The accent contract

Each card exposes a single custom property, --c. Every piece of chrome downstream is color-mixed off it: the border tint, the outer bloom, the specular sheen, the poster well and the buttons. Change --c and the whole card re-skins with no other edits.

The rebind gotcha

Custom properties compute on the element that declares them, so any node that sets its own --c must re-declare the --glass-card-* recipes or it inherits root cyan. .pdx-glass-rebind does that rebind for you: put it on any element that sets a local accent.

Aliases

--_c and --dc exist for components that took an accent under a different name. They resolve to the same value, so a component can keep its old prop name without a second code path.

Never a hand-picked hex

Point --c at the surface's real token: --day-*, --cat-* or --board-*. A literal hex breaks calm mode, because calm flattens the tokens and cannot reach a hardcoded value.

02 · Anatomy, layer by layer

All of these are surface only: fill, edge, bloom, sheen, button chrome. Layout, spacing, structural radius and type scale come from the other token files, not from the glass layer.

Slab · §1.1

--glass-card-bg is a two-stop radial: pure black core fading to a faint accent wash at the rim, plus an accent uplight from below. --glass-card-border is a neon edge, never a flat grey line. --glass-card-shadow stacks a 2px black ring, a deep drop, two accent blooms scaled by --dir-gm, and inset highlights. Radius 14px, backdrop blur 12px.

Sheen · §1.2

Two overlays, always on. --glass-sheen is a 133 degree white diagonal gloss at the top left; --glass-sheen-specular is a warm accent-tinted hot spot at the bottom right. They are what make the slab read as glass rather than matte.

Refract seam · §1.3

A full-spectrum bar on the shared animated seam engine, --refract-seam-height (2px) by default. Same bar as the header and footer seams; on a card it sits as a top hairline inset 7px from each edge.

Poster / logo well · §1.4

Where a card carries an image it sits in the well: --poster-well-bg (accent-tinted radial floor), a 4px solid accent floor line (--poster-well-floor) and a faint scanline (--poster-well-scan). Events use it as a poster thumb. The Directory does not: it softens the idea into a blurred logo orb with no floor and no box.

Buttons · §1.5

Primary actions are glass pills, radius 9px. Three states: default (near-black translucent fill, 2px black ring, accent hairline, inward sheen, no outer bloom), --solid (filled accent, black text, the one loud CTA per card) and --outline (secondary). Buttons never carry the card's outer bloom; that separation is deliberate.

Neutral · §1.1b

When a card has no semantic accent, pdx-glass-card--neutral drives the same slab from silver --glass-neutral-c2. pdx-glass-card--left-accent adds a coloured left rail for list rows.

03 · The surfaces

What changes between them is only --c. The colour tokens themselves are defined in the Colour group; cards just aim the accent at the right one.

Events · EventCard

A horizontal list row keyed to the event's day colour, so each row glows in its day. An 84px poster thumb in the well, a day-coloured left border, and the RSVP and attendance clusters. Full row spec in EVENTS_GUIDE.md.

Directory · PlaceCard

A masonry card keyed to the place's category neon. It swaps the poster for a soft neon logo orb, and reveals Follow and Share only on hover so the resting state stays clean. Nonprofits, healthcare, real estate, campgrounds and groups each take a specialty --_edge gradient instead of a flat accent border.

Ads

The promoted units run the same shell through pdx-glass-rebind plus sheen, accented by the event's day colour, then layer on the slideshow and countdown. Their in-feed cousins live in the Hub and feed panel.

Boards

Gig Board, Gifting and Missed Connections use the shell with the board accent and the warmer board paper text. Missed Connections renders quote-style. Board anatomy around the feed lives in the Patterns band.

Queued · Housing

The Housing board ships on this same shell in a future update. It needs nothing new: it is the deep-glass card with --c pointed at --board-housing, which is now defined as the softened cyan the boards specimen already uses for it. Listing shape follows the Gig Board pattern (a two-way board: people with a room and people looking for one), so it should reuse the give and iso kinds rather than inventing a third.

Do not give Housing its own card, its own radius or its own button treatment. If it needs something the shell cannot express, that is a change to the shell.

Naming

The code still calls the Missed Connections board "spotted" internally: the /spotted route, SpottedCard, SpottedDetailModal. That is a legacy identifier only. The product name, everywhere a user sees it and everywhere in design, is Missed Connections.

One card, one scroll

There are no levels. Opening a card gives you one slab that scrolls internally: poster, title, the info block, flags, then lineup and talent, latest from the host, the calendar and message actions, the I'll be there / Missed connections tabs, the requests and the unclaimed warning. The scroll happens inside the card, so the shell, seam, sheen and header chips never move while the content passes under them.

The footer never leaves

Event info → and I'll be there are pinned below the scroll area, so the two things a user came to do are reachable from any position in the card. Event info jumps down to that part of the same scroll rather than opening anything new. Share and close sit above the poster and stay put too.

Nothing gets dropped

Everything the closed card cannot hold lives further down the same scroll rather than on a separate page: host updates, talent tags, the going list, Missed Connections for that night, and the three data-integrity links. An unclaimed listing always ends with the orange warning, since sourced details can be wrong.

Directory tabs are pills

A place packs more relations than an event, so the Directory modal ends in a pill row: Events, Past with a count, Missed connections, Gigs. Pills, not the underlined tabs, because the set grows and can wrap. The selected pill takes magenta, matching Claim this business above it. Every tab shows a plain empty line when it has nothing, never a blank panel.

One object, two sizes

Tapping opens the matching modal: an event opens EventModal, a Directory place opens PlaceModal, a Missed Connection opens its detail modal. The modal reuses that card's --c, the glass shell and the sheen, then adds the full poster, the meta block and the actions. Closed card and open modal read as one thing at two sizes, not two designs.

Scroll lock

Modals lock scroll the iOS-safe way: a position:fixed lock, never overflow:hidden, which detaches the fixed bottom nav in iOS Safari.

06 · Motion

Entrance

pgDirCardIn, a fade up staggered by 40ms times --i down the grid, with backwards fill so it never locks the hover transform.

Hover

A 2 to 6px lift, a small brightness and saturation bump, and the border tightens toward the accent. No infinite bloom pulse at rest: it caused scroll jank on dense lists.

Calm

--dir-gm, the resting glow multiplier, zeroes out, so blooms flatten while the slab and edge stay legible. Entrance and sheen animation stop. Toggle calm in the sidebar to watch the cards above.

08 · Do and don't

Do

Set --c and add pdx-glass-rebind on any node that declares its own accent. Point it at the surface's real token. Use --solid for the one loud CTA per card and keep the rest default or outline. Let the modal inherit the card's accent.

Don't

Don't ship a flat slab or a plain grey border: that is the old lite-glass look, and deep glass replaced it. Don't put the card's outer bloom on a button. Don't set a local --c without rebinding. Don't hardcode a colour the accent tokens already carry, and don't call the Missed Connections board "Spotted".
