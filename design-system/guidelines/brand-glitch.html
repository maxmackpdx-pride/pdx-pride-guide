Glitch

Two tinted copies of the wordmark sit behind it on screen blend and jump for a handful of frames roughly every three seconds, then rest. It runs on the nav mark and the hero wordmark only. Never on body copy, never on a control, never more than one on screen.

Slow RGB glitch, nav and hero wordmark

Cycle 3.2s, steps(1) so the jumps are hard cuts rather than tweens.
Active window 86 to 97 percent, about 350ms of the cycle.
Offsets never exceed 3px.
Ghost A hue-rotate 150deg, ghost B hue-rotate -95deg, both at saturate 2.2 on mix-blend-mode: screen.
Calm mode and reduced motion remove both ghosts, leaving the clean mark.
