Copy rules

How Zaylist sounds, in one place. Straightforward with a slight bro lean: plainspoken, low-key, a little dry. Short sentences. Say the thing, then stop. It talks to the reader as "you" and to the community as "we" and "each other," and it never pretends to be a company.

Write this

"Doors at 9. Cash for the door, cards at the bar. Back room opens after the first set."

"Sober space. No alcohol served, BYO not allowed."

"Here's the week. Pull up."

Not this

"Get ready for an unforgettable night of pure magic!"

"A safe space for everyone to be their authentic selves."

"Join our vibrant community of amazing humans."

Lead with the practical fact: time, door, price, access.
Use the community's own words for itself. Never soften them.
Sentence case for body. Uppercase only in display and chrome.
Ranges read "Jul 16 to 19", never with a dash glyph.
No em dashes. Anywhere. Periods, commas, colons, parentheses, or "to".
No exclamation marks in UI text, and no hype adjectives.
No corporate euphemism: authentic, vibrant, journey, curated.
No protest or activist framing in our own voice. Listings keep their own language.

The name is the joke

Said out loud, Zaylist sounds like the list, the fancy one you want to be on. The pun lives in the phrasing, not the spelling: build the sentence so the reader hears it. Closest thing the brand has to a tagline mechanic, so it earns a place in heroes, promoter pitches, submission confirmations and share cards.

You're on Zaylist Is your party on Zaylist? Get on Zaylist
Always spelled Zaylist. One word, one capital, no apostrophe.
Set it up with "on" or "your": that is what makes the ear hear "the list".
Never respell it to force the joke: no Zay'List, Zay List or ZayList.
One per screen. Twice is a bit, and never in nav, buttons, forms or legal.

Money and placement

What we promise about cost and ranking has to survive the day we start selling something. Describe what is true now, never what will be true forever.

Do say

Free to browse

Labeled

Human review

Ranking not for sale

Don't say

Free forever

Never any paid placement

Fixed prices, until you are ready to hold them

Say what a thing costs today. "Free to browse" is a fact, "free forever" is a promise.
"Ranking not for sale" is the line that holds even after we sell placement, because paid slots get labeled.
Label anything paid, and say a human reviewed it.
Do not invent products on the site before they exist: owned listing, boost, tiers, none of it.
Do not publish a price we are not ready to hold.
The pitch form is the exception. It can stay open-ended and ask what someone wants.

Scene vernacular

Dry does not mean flat. "Yas" and "gurl" are on brand and land well in stickers, empty states, toasts and board taglines. One per screen at most, and never inside functional UI: no buttons, labels, nav items or form errors. If it would be embarrassing on an error message, it does not go there.

Patterns

Three-beat mantras

Keep it free · keep it kind · keep it moving

Stay kind · stay anonymous · reveal when ready

Board taglines and close seams. Separated by the middot, never by a dash.

Casing

UI chrome (nav, buttons, chips, badges, section titles, counts) is all caps in the condensed display face. Body copy is sentence case. Handles stay lowercase.
