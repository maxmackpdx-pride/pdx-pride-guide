Mobile nav

Mobile carries two bars, and they split the work. The top bar holds identity: where you are in the site, your alerts and your account. The bottom dock holds movement: the five places you actually go. Neither one duplicates the other, and the dock is portaled to <body> so iOS Safari cannot clip it.

Top bar

Replaces the desktop nav row under 1024px. Home and About as a segmented pair on the left, then a spacer, then the alert bolt and the avatar cluster. Signed out, the cluster collapses to a single JOIN button.

Segmented pair

Home and About sit in a debossed #111 track with a #2b2b2b edge and a 9px radius, not a pill. The active one fills --neon-cyan, flips to black text and takes a raised inset highlight; idle is #777. Signed out, the same control shape carries the --neon-magenta JOIN pill on the right. Ported from hub-shell.css .hub-mtop__mode and goes white on hover.

Alert bolt

A lightning glyph on #1a1a1f with a #333 edge. With anything pending it takes a red-tinted border, #ff8ab8 text and a soft red glow, plus a count chip. Tapping opens a notification panel, at most 320px wide and 70vh tall, anchored right.

Avatar

39px here rather than the 55px default, with a 24px caret beside it. Same flag-ring bloom as everywhere else. It opens the same account menu as desktop.

One overlay at a time

Opening the notify panel, the account menu, a tab sheet or the inbox dismisses the others through a single dismiss event. Two overlays can never stack.

Bottom dock

Places, Events, Hub, Boards, Messages. Events and Boards open sheets instead of navigating; Hub carries the Z mark; Messages opens the floating inbox. Hub and admin shells no longer render their own bars, so this is the only mobile bottom bar on the site.

Why sheets

Beaches folds into the Events sheet rather than taking a sixth tab. Boards holds the three community boards. Opening one dismisses the other overlays through a single dismiss event, so a sheet, the notify menu and the inbox can never stack.

Specification

Rail

Fixed, full width, z-index 80, transparent, no top border, pointer-events:none. Padding 10px 10px calc(8px + safe-area). The rail is a hit-test hole; only the dock takes input.

Dock chip

Max width 520px, centered, radius 22px, rgba(8,8,10,.94) under a 4% white radial sheen, 1px solid #000 plus 0 0 0 1px #000, lift shadow 0 -4px 28px rgba(0,0,0,.55), inset top highlight 6%.

Tab

Min height 56px, radius 14px, 1px solid #000 at every state, idle text #6e6e76 on 2% white, display font 700 at 10.5px, 0.06em, uppercase. Icons 26px at 2.3 stroke.

Active

Fill is color-mix(accent 16%, #0a0a0e), 18% for cyan and violet. Two-stop neon glow at 70 to 75% and 45 to 50%. Border stays black. Never a colored border, never an outer ring.

Accents

Places cyan #19e3ff, Events orange #ff6600, Hub cyan, Boards lime #ccff00, Messages pink #ff00cc. Green #39ff14 and violet exist for hub-shell tabs. Fixed per tab, never day colors.

Center tab

Hub is the Z mark at 24px sitting on its own 3px spectrum bar, never a house icon. The mark is nudged down so the gap above it, the gap to the bar and the gap to the HUB label read evenly rather than top-heavy. Signed out it opens the auth modal instead of routing.

Badge

Messages carries the attention count: pink chip, dark text, top:-8px right:-12px, capped at 9+. It sums unread DMs plus the admin queue plus the owner desk.

Calm mode

Calm and reduced motion drop the glow on active tabs and hold the fill. Nothing pulses.

Never

No sitewide cyan pull above the bar: that was retired. The grip belongs to the hub drawer only. Do not add a sixth tab, and do not give the dock a neon edge.
