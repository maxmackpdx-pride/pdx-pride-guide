D · Patterns · Homepage · Founder direction 2026-08-03

Homepage front door

The homepage begins with one full-bleed Welcome scene, followed by one horizontal, no-wrap rail of seven Zaylist destination cards. The rail is a deliberate section with its own padding, modest gaps, and dividers, not a full-page carousel or wrapping desktop grid. On motion-capable desktop it flows slowly and seamlessly from right to left; mobile, Calm Mode, and reduced motion use manual scrolling with snap points.

Shared contract

Welcome first. The wordmark and two direct actions occupy the opening viewport. The wordmark keeps the slow RGB glitch, which Calm Mode and reduced motion remove. The real site navigation remains outside this pattern and is never recreated inside it.

Nude Beaches leads. Current founder direction places Nude Beaches first, followed by Events, Places, Haüsing, Gifting, Gig Board and Missed Connections.

One destination rail. All seven homepage destination cards live in a single horizontal nowrap track between clear section dividers. Use modest, consistent gaps and preserve section padding before the first card and after the last. Never split the destinations into multiple rows or add a second visible marquee.

Slow desktop flow. On motion-capable desktop, the rail advances continuously from right to left at a calm, readable pace and wraps without a visible jump. Keep one semantic seven-card set. If seamless rendering requires clones, they are presentational only: aria-hidden, unfocusable, inert, and excluded from analytics and accessible reading order.

No hidden mobile content. Phones disable autoplay and use native horizontal touch scrolling with mandatory or proximity snap points. Every destination keeps its title, short explanation and action. The rail owns horizontal overflow; the page itself never does.

One accent per world. Each world rebinds the deep-glass shell through --c. Bloom stays local to the surface. Calm Mode removes ambient video and bloom without removing content.

Fluent 2 is an undertone. World cards may use a quiet inner highlight, composed 16px corner, low elevation, and a one-pixel hover lift. Zaylist deep glass, color roles, type, seams, and motifs remain dominant.

Wallpaper overlay stays behind. A light, transparent static/scan texture may cover the homepage wallpaper and occasionally displace by a few pixels as a subtle glitch. It sits behind every content object, never intercepts input, and must not reduce text, logo, control, or focus-ring legibility. Calm Mode and reduced motion keep the texture visible but completely static.

Depth starts below the texture. Build the homepage background from layered black and dark-gray shadow planes beneath the static/scan overlay and beneath all content. On capable desktop, the planes may move at subtly different parallax rates to create depth. Cards, headings, text, controls, contained objects, and the texture itself do not inherit that movement. Mobile uses a stable lightweight still composition; Calm Mode and reduced motion remove parallax entirely.

Counter owns the hero seam. Place the counter strip immediately after the main Zaylist hero and before the destination-card rail, with its bottom edge aligned exactly to the hero end. The strip remains transparent over one vertical gradient that is 100% transparent at the top and 80% black at the bottom. Put the rainbow divider directly below the counter; do not leave an extra band, gap, or second divider between counter and rail.

01 · Orange

Nude beaches

Live Rooster Rock air, water, wind and river conditions occupy the first product position. A low-contrast neutral gray topographic motif sits behind the utility; orange remains reserved for live state and action.

02 · Day colour

Up next events

The outer Events world is the flyer stage. Do not inset another card or reserve separate header and footer bands. The flyer covers the surface from top edge to bottom edge; the world header, event information, counter, and position rail overlay that image. When local event data is empty, use the repository's canonical demo event. Calm Mode and reduced motion stop automatic advance.

03 · Cyan

Places

A compact live-directory app-tile launcher sits over the quiet Portland street-and-river map. Remove two rows from the former grid at each breakpoint: larger screens use 8 columns by 3 rows; phones use 5 columns by 6 rows. Render only the visible capacity, keep source order stable, and align the footer/action datum with sibling destination cards. Tiles show the directory logo or initials and open the business directly.

04–07 · Object motifs

Community worlds

Haüsing uses a house floor plan, Gifting uses gift-box construction drawings, Gigs uses a pinned bulletin board, and Missed Connections uses lost/found/looking notices. These four motifs run edge-to-edge across the complete card at a visibly stronger register than the restrained river and Portland map layers.

Rail

One line, one sequence

Destination cards never wrap. Use a flex or grid auto-column rail, section-level padding and dividers, modest gaps, predictable card widths, and one scroll container. The seven-card track may use inert presentational clones only to close the seamless desktop loop; never expose a second semantic set or second visible rail.

Mobile / Calm

Snap, do not autoplay

Disable continuous flow on mobile, Calm Mode, and reduced motion. Cards stay readable and keep 44px minimum actions. Snap each card to the rail's padded start edge, allow the next card to peek when space permits, contain overscroll, and verify the document has no horizontal overflow.

NEXT preview

Six arrive, one stack stays

Use one real six-card NEXT set only. Three cards enter simultaneously from the left and three simultaneously from the right, then crash-land into one centered, deliberately overlapping stack and remain there. Preserve each card's own logo, accent and contained object; the shared stack creates continuity without cloning or blending product identities.

NEXT section head

Exact copy, one red word

Place the rainbow divider immediately above the section. The heading copy is exactly SEE WHAT I’M BUILDING NEXT. in uppercase, including the final period. Only NEXT. is red; the rest of the heading retains the standard high-contrast heading color. Do not tint the full line or move the divider into the card stack.

NEXT section field

Trail-map blueprint ghosts

Fill the complete NEXT section with one clipped topographic/trail-map blueprint field and oversized ghosts drawn from the NEXT product-logo family. Keep every background element monochrome and low opacity, below heading and card content, with a protected reading lane. This panel has no parallax at any breakpoint: the map and logo ghosts remain stable while the cards arrive. Mobile uses a simplified authored crop; Calm Mode and reduced motion preserve the same still field.

Calm / reduced motion

Show the answer first

Skip travel and impact entirely. Render the final centered stack on first paint with the same six cards, order, overlap, labels and actions. Do not replace the sequence with a marquee, repeated cards, or a flattened collage.

Wallpaper overlay

Signal, never obstruction

Use one low-opacity static/scan layer below all content. An occasional short glitch displacement may offset only that texture; content, controls, cards, and contained objects do not jump with it. Set it pointer-free and freeze displacement under Calm Mode or reduced motion.

Background depth

Shadow planes below signal

Layer restrained black and dark-gray shapes below the texture overlay. Desktop may apply slow, low-distance parallax to those planes only. Keep contrast and displacement subtle, preserve a stable reading field, and render a still low-cost version on mobile, Calm Mode, and reduced motion.

Hero seam

Counter, then rainbow

The counter closes the hero and introduces the rail. Keep the strip itself transparent and use one vertical gradient behind its content: transparent at the top and rgba(0,0,0,.8) at the bottom. Align its bottom with the hero boundary and place the rainbow divider immediately underneath. On mobile, distribute cells equally, preserve readable labels and values, and measure that no cell, divider, hero copy, or following rail overlaps.
