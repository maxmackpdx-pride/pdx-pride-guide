Motion, loaders & seams

Every timing, loader and divider in production, ported value for value. Motion here is ambient and additive: slow loops that idle, snappy 150ms responses to touch, and one flowing seam that doubles as the page loader. Calm mode and reduced motion silence all of it, and both are treated as a real state, not an afterthought.

Ported from client/src/components/ds/tokens/effects.css · SpectrumLoader.tsx · BoardLoadingState.tsx · PullToRefresh.tsx · UserAvatar.tsx · index.css (.rainbow-bar, .user-avatar, .spectrum-loader)

01 · Foundations

Three easings and four durations cover the whole site. Anything that reacts to a finger uses --dur-fast; anything that idles uses --dur-pulse.

ease-out · .2,.8,.2,1
spring · .34,1.56,.64,1
ease-inout · .65,0,.35,1

Durations

--dur-fast 150ms snappy hover and press. --dur-base 220ms default. --dur-slow 360ms panels. --dur-pulse 4s ambient.

Interaction

--hover-lift -2px and --press-scale .97. Buttons lift up and left on hover, press down and right. Cards lift 2px.

Entrance ladder

Hero .48s, stats band at +.07s, feeds .35s, compose panels .25s, modals .2s. Scroll reveal fades sections in on first view.

Never

No entrance on a resting glass card's bloom, no bounce on data, and no animation that cannot be removed by calm mode.

02 · Page dividers, the seam engine

One bar does all the dividing: under the header, above the footer, between sections, and across the top of clickable cards. Cyan to yellow to magenta to orange, five stops on a 200% track so it can flow forever.

Default · 3px · under the header
Thick · 2.7px full bleed · above the footer

Three animations run together at 3.4s: pdxSeamFlow slides the gradient, pdxSeamGlow breathes the box-shadow from cyan to magenta, and a 24% wide white ::after rides pdxSeamGlint on mix-blend-mode:screen from translateX(-165%) to 150%. The whole bar sits at opacity .8. Calm and reduced motion swap to a flat --rainbow-bar, hide the glint and pin a tight static shadow: visible, still, never gone.

02.2Seam ChargeRoute + fetch loader

The header seam is the page loader. While the router is navigating or a query is fetching, the softened gradient (#c8fa3c, #19e3ff, #ff1fa0, #ff8c00) fills with pdxSeamFill to 68% of the timeline, the glint sweeps, and pdxSeamFlash blinks the bar white at 74% as it completes. No spinner, no top-of-page progress bar: the divider already there does the work.

03.3Pull to refreshInstalled PWA only
dragging · 60%
refreshing

Standalone display mode hides the browser's own gesture, so the app ships its own. Pull from the very top: 72px of resolved travel triggers it, capped at 110px with 0.5 resistance. The chip is 36px of rgba(10,10,12,.92) with a 14% white edge; inside, an 18px ring with a cyan top rotates with the drag, then spins at ptr-spin .7s linear while every query refetches, and settles 450ms after. Touch devices in standalone only, so it can never double up with the browser.

05 · Ambient loops

The slow layer. Each of these idles forever at around 4s and reads as breathing, not blinking.

pdxPulse reads a per-element --dc (the day or accent color) and breathes the glow from 18% to 32%. On deep-glass surfaces the resting bloom is already baked into the card token, so the pulse is hover and emphasis only, never the resting state. pdxSpotPulse is the fixed magenta version for missed-connection cards. The cyan grip belongs to the hub drawer alone: the sitewide version above the mobile bar was retired.

06 · Flag ring glow

The ring is not a ring. It is a soft flag-colored bloom sitting behind the photo, with a light sweep that orbits the halo. Nothing is ever drawn on top of a face.

06.1Ring bloom + shimmerIdle 5s · hover 1.9s

Two layers, both at z-index:-1 inside an isolated stacking context. ::before is the flag itself as a conic or linear gradient, inset -6.5%, blurred by --avatar-size * .044 at saturate(1.2) brightness(1.12), held at opacity .86, running pdxaShimmer 5s linear to rotate and avatar-glow-breathe 2.4s to fade between .58 and 1. The highlight is a separate conic (transparent to 40deg, rgba(255,255,255,.66) at 58deg, transparent after 92deg) blurred by --avatar-size * .024 at opacity .78, radial-masked to the halo band so it can never cross the photo. Hover speeds the bloom to 2.2s and 1.6s at saturate(1.28) brightness(1.22), and the sweep to 1.9s at brightness(1.65). Chain swaps to a cooler steel highlight at 3.6s, 1.4s on hover, and carries the padlock charm.

No Glow (ring="none") renders neither layer: no bloom, no sweep, and no shimmer element in the DOM at all. Calm mode and reduced motion set animation:none on both layers and calm additionally greys them to grayscale(1) at opacity .45, while the photo keeps its color. The ring is identity, so it stays visible under calm even when it stops moving.

Code note: pdxRingShimmer exists in effects.css but nothing in the client references it. The live motion is pdxaShimmer plus avatar-glow-breathe. Treat the former as an orphan keyframe, not the spec.

Live Wave is a four-bar equalizer scaling from .22 to 1 for anything happening right now. Count It Up spins stat numerals on reveal with pdxNumPop, and pdxFloatUp carries a delta upward as it fades. RSVP Sparks throws a handful of lime dots along --tx and --ty when you join a night: confirmation you can feel, on the press only. Split-Flap is JS-driven rotateX flipping, used for the footer sign-off.

07.2Logo glitchLoad · idle ~3s

The real wordmark art, not type. Two hue-rotated copies of assets/logo-lockup.png sit over the base on mix-blend-mode:screen and tear for a few frames roughly every three seconds, then rest. Because the ghosts are the artwork itself, the glitch survives any change to the logo. Calm mode and reduced motion remove the ghost layers entirely and leave the clean mark.

Code note: GlitchWord.tsx and GlitchLogo.tsx on master still target the old "PRIDE" string. They should glitch ZAYLIST or be removed.

08 · Calm mode

Calm is a first-class state, not a degradation. [data-calm="true"] and prefers-reduced-motion are handled together.

Stops

Grain, glitch, the aurora orbs, tickers, ambient glows, entrance animation, the hero background video (poster still instead), and the RSVP pin pulse.

Freezes

Seams stay visible but static, with a tight edge shadow and no glint. Card top seams flatten to a plain rainbow bar.

Flattens

Day colors drop to grey so color can never be the only signal, and map chip outlines drop to #555.

Keeps

Flag rings, greyed but present. They are meaning, not decoration. Photos keep their color; only chrome greys.
