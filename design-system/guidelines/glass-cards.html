Glass surface & card system

The 2026 source of truth for every enclosing surface. One shared token, one accent per surface. Cards, tiles, the floating inbox, map islands, project rows and nav all draw from it. Set --_c to the surface's neon (day, category, or board color) and the OLED core, tinted edge, glow, vignette and refraction seam follow.

Anatomy
1. Near-black OLED core. Radial, black at center to a faint accent tint at the base.
2. Bottom accent bloom rising into the core.
3. Thin 1px neon edge (accent 55% over #101014) sitting over a 2px black outline.
4. Double outer neon glow, inner top highlight, and an inward hole-rim vignette.
5. Top-left specular sheen and a bottom-right accent bloom (the ::before / ::after layers).
6. Prismatic refraction seam on the top edge. Add <span class="pdx-refract"> as the first child.

Usage: apply .pdx-glass to any container and set --_c. Keep overflow:visible so the glow and seam bleed. All motion and glow silence under calm mode and reduced-motion. This replaces the flat --surface-card + brutalist-offset treatment on enclosing surfaces; type, color, spacing and the sticker / marker primitives are unchanged.
