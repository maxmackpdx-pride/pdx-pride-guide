Zaylist Design System

A high-energy neon zine with a utility backbone: welcoming, scannable, and community-first. Anti-corporate by design, and proudly solo-run: built by one person for the PDX scene, not a sponsor.

This design system powers Zaylist (zaylist.com), a community-run guide to Portland's queer events. It covers the event directory and schedule, the Queer Directory, Nude Beaches, the community boards (Spotted!, Gifting, Gig Board), the promoter hub, member profiles, the member Hub, and the admin dashboard.

CONTENT FUNDAMENTALS (voice & copy)

The voice is straightforward with a slight bro lean: plainspoken, low-key, a little dry. Short sentences. Say the thing, then stop. It talks to the reader as "you" and to the community as "we" / "each other." It is also openly one person's project: "Built by one person in Portland. No committee, no corporate parent. Just someone who loves this scene." and "Portland, Oregon. Made by Tucker, for the community, not shareholders."

  • Register: casual and direct, never breathless or promotional. "Here's the week." "Tickets are at the door." "Pull up." No exclamation stacking, no hype adjectives, no marketing verbs. Cheeky is fine, corny is not.
  • Scene vernacular is allowed, in small doses. "Yas" and "gurl" are on-brand and land well in stickers, empty states, toasts and board taglines. One per screen at most, and never inside functional UI (buttons, labels, nav, form errors).
  • Tone: warm and practical. Signature sign-off energy: "Take care of each other."
  • No protest or activist framing in our own voice. Zaylist copy (hero, section blurbs, buttons, stickers, footer, FAQ answers) does not editorialize or take a stance. Event listings are the exception: an event keeps its real name and its own language, so "Dyke March", "Trans March" and a member's post about a hand-painted sign all stay as written. The rule applies to what we say, not to what we list.
  • Casing: UI chrome (nav, buttons, chips, badges, section titles, counts) is ALL CAPS in the condensed display face. Body copy is sentence case.
  • Kickers come in two registers. Zine kicker: a lime chip in Barlow ("PORTLAND FEST 2026 · COMMUNITY SUBMISSIONS"). Utility kicker: small monospace, 0.2em tracking, colored per section, often with a blinking live dot.
  • Three-beat mantras, separated by "·", are the board tagline pattern: "Keep it free · keep it kind · keep it moving" (Gifting), "Stay kind · stay anonymous · reveal when ready" (Missed Connections), "Need work? Need help? Both belong here." (Gig Board), "Submit it. Claim it. Keep Portland queer." (Promoters).
  • Close seams: board-style pages end with a thin seam plus a mantra and the page URL, e.g. "Show up. Spend queer. Keep them alive." + zaylist.com/directory.
  • Slogans / stickers: loud, irreverent, collage-y: "KEEP PORTLAND WEIRD", "FUCK META", "MADE BY THE SCENE", "YAS GURL". Flair, never functional labels. Irreverent is fine, political is not.
  • The name is a pun, use it. Said out loud, Zaylist sounds like "the list," the fancy one you want to be on. The joke lives in the phrasing, not the spelling: "You're on Zaylist." "Is your party on Zaylist?" "Get on Zaylist." Setting it up with "on" or "your" is what makes the ear hear it. It is the closest thing we have to a tagline mechanic, so it earns a place in heroes, promoter pitches, submission confirmations and share cards. Rules: it is always spelled Zaylist, one word, one capital, no apostrophe. Never respell it to force the joke (no Zay'List, Zay List, ZayList), and keep it to one instance per screen so it stays a wink instead of a bit.
  • Event copy stays tight and scannable: title, venue, when-line ("Fri, Jul 17 · 9:00 PM · Pearl District"), a few tags. No fluff.
  • Emoji: essentially none in the UI. A single sparkle "✦" appears as a decorative separator. Day and status meaning is carried by color, not emoji.
  • No em dashes, ever. Use periods, commas, colons, parentheses, or "to" for ranges ("July 13 to 19"). Hard project rule (see CLAUDE.md and docs/LIVE_DESIGN_STANDARD.md).

VISUAL FOUNDATIONS

One card shell

Every card on the site is the same object: a near-black OLED slab with a neon edge, a glass sheen, a rainbow refract seam and a soft outer bloom. It is called deep glass. A surface does not get its own card, it gets the shell pointed at its own accent through a single custom property, --c, which every piece of chrome downstream is color-mixed off. Events aim it at the day colour, the Directory at the category neon, boards at the board accent. Any node that sets its own --c must also carry .pdx-glass-rebind, or it silently inherits root cyan. Full anatomy, the four surfaces, the modal as the open state of the same card, and the token table are on the Card system card under Brand.

Two layers, one brand

The 2026 site runs two registers of the same identity. Pick the layer first, then style within it.

  1. Zine layer: heroes, events, marketing moments. Near-black base, pure neons, brutalist offset shadows, collage photo heroes under scrims and grain, glitch wordmarks, sticker tags. Loud.
  2. Utility layer ("board makeover"): boards, Nude Beaches, About, Hub, Admin, stats. Ink panels (#050505 / #06060a / #070708 bands, #0c0c0f cards) with #1c1c22 borders, monospace kickers and labels, softened panel neons (#c8fa3c lime, #19e3ff cyan, #ff1fa0 magenta, #ff8c00 orange, #b06bff purple), tabular numerals, restrained glows, radius up to 10 to 12px on data tiles. Calm, dense, legible.

Core rules

  • Base: always near-black #0a0a0a. Zine content sits on #0b0b0b cards with 2px solid #2b2b2b borders, radius 0 to 6px. Utility cards sit on #0c0c0f / #0d0d10 with #1c1c22 / #24242c hairlines, radius 6 to 12px. Pills and avatars are the only fully-round shapes.
  • One neon per element. Acid yellow #CCFF00 is the default action colour, not the only one. Five surface families own their own primary and set it with a single data-context attribute that rebinds --accent and --c for the whole subtree: Hub and settings cyan (--primary-hub), admin and moderation magenta (--primary-admin), owner desk violet (--primary-owner), Rooster Rock orange (--primary-rooster), Sauvie Island acid green (--primary-sauvie). Everything else falls back to --primary. Cyan is also the link and focus colour everywhere, including inside the hub, so links stay underlined there. Never reach for a neon directly in a component: read --accent.
  • One green. --green-acid #39FF14 is the only green in the system. The old #00EE44 is retired everywhere (status ramp, cafés category, avatar presence dot, Collins Beach pins, two line icons); --neon-green survives only as a deprecated alias of --green-acid.
  • Day color system (the rule that matters most). Every event belongs to one event day, and that day drives its color everywhere (map pin, filter pill, day tag, card glow, schedule column underline). MON #8800FF, TUE #0044FF, WED #FFEE00, THU #00FFFF, FRI #FF00CC, SAT #39FF14, SUN #FF6600. MON and TUE have lighter text variants (--day-mon-text, --day-tue-text). Unknown day = white (--day-unknown); multi-day venues use the --day-multi gradient (map pins render a pie, one slice per day, MON to SUN order). Colors are data, not decoration; always use the --day-* tokens so calm mode can flatten them to grey. #CCFF00 is never a day color.
  • Admission tags: FREE --tag-free (a softened lime plate, not a solid acid block: Free is the most common admission, and the rule is one solid fill per card), TICKETED cyan --tag-ticketed, DONATION amber #FFB23D --tag-donation.
  • Schedule accents: schedule grid cards do NOT use day colors (the column is the day); they cycle --schedule-accent-1..5 (#19E3FF, #FF6600, #39FF14, #A855F7, #FF00CC).
  • Directory categories: each place type has a category neon (--cat-bars magenta, --cat-food orange, --cat-cafes acid green, --cat-venues cyan, --cat-services violet, --cat-shops amber, --cat-hotels blue) used as the card's accent and its map pin. It drives the whole deep-glass shell (border tint, bloom, sheen, logo orb), not a 3px top border: that was the old lite-glass card. Nonprofit, healthcare, real estate, campgrounds and groups take a specialty --_edge gradient instead of a flat accent border. The map key adds a rainbow conic swatch for queer-owned.
  • Community board accents. Board identity via --board-* tokens: Missed Connections! magenta, Gifting acid yellow, Gigs violet, and Housing softened cyan (--board-housing, queued: the board ships on the same deep-glass shell in a future update, reusing the Gig Board give/ISO pattern). Board accents are identity, so a data-context scope never repaints them. On utility surfaces these soften to #ff1fa0 / lime / #b06bff (tab underlines, running heads). Boards also carry warmer paper text: --board-text #f4f1ea, --board-muted #bdbab2.
  • Status ramp (live-conditions tiles): good #39FF14, warn #FFB347, bad #FF4D4D, neutral #19E3FF, as 3px top borders on data cards.
  • Text: headings #fff; body #e6e2d9 (muted #c8c4bb); meta #999; faint #666. Links cyan.
  • Type: display is Barlow Condensed (700 to 900, uppercase, tight ~0.95, down to 0.82 for stat numerals); body is Inter; and monospace is now an official third voice (ui-monospace stack) for utility kickers, running-head nav, stat labels, and meta chips: 10 to 12px, 0.14 to 0.2em tracking, uppercase. Big page titles on utility chrome (.page-header__title) use a lighter weight with a 1px black text-stroke and hard 2px shadow over paper white #f7f5ee.
  • Backgrounds & texture: a sitewide film-grain overlay (SVG turbulence, opacity ~0.12) plus a fixed body wash of three faint neon radials and 1px scanlines. Zine pages add a chartreuse halftone dot grid (.zine-page). Photo heroes stack in fixed order: bg photo (saturate 1.08, contrast 1.04) → optional webm overlay videos stacked by preset from client/src/lib/heroOverlays.ts (light leaks screen .308 at 0.5x, film grain overlay .202 at 0.85x, scanlines screen .09, confetti screen .2 at 0.75x, rain screen .26; home = leaks + grain + confetti, panel and atmosphere = leaks + grain) → legibility scrim → grain → content. Imagery reads warm, nighttime-saturated, heavy grain.

Signature effects

  • Lit plate and floor bloom (replaces the brutalist offset). Anything that used to carry the flat 4px 4px 0 magenta offset now reads as a lit fill on a black keyline, a 1px highlight along the top edge, and the element's own accent blooming on the floor beneath it: --sticker-lit for posters, markers, tape and collage, --chrome-* for product chrome. Hover grows the bloom, press collapses it to a press shade. --brutal-shadow is deprecated and kept only so old markup does not break. Calm mode zeroes the bloom and leaves the keyline and bevel.
  • Animated rainbow seam. The rainbow bar (cyan → yellow → magenta → orange; thin 2px, thick 2.7px) now flows continuously with a traveling white glint and a soft glow pulse, under the header, above the footer, and between sections. As Seam Charge, the header seam doubles as the route/page loader (the nav drives it while navigating or fetching). Utility page-headers use a softened seam gradient (#c8fa3c, #19e3ff, #ff1fa0, #ff8c00).
  • Soft neon glow 0 0 14px color-mix(<accent> 18%) on cards with a slow ~4s pulse; hover brightens. Missed Connections cards pulse magenta.
  • Sticker outline: big display numerals (countdown) carry an off-center black outline over the neon.
  • Glitch: the ZAYLIST wordmark and accent words run a slow, chunky RGB glitch: two tinted copies on mix-blend-mode: screen, hard steps(1) cuts, a 3.2s cycle with roughly 350ms of activity and offsets never above 3px. Off in calm/reduced motion. (See KNOWN CODE GAPS: the glitch components on master still target the old "PRIDE" string.)
  • Neon tape (Resume, collage moments): neon strips rotated ±0.7 to 1.6° with dark ink text, a lit bevel and a floor bloom. The tilt stays, the flat offset is gone. Lime "HIRE ME" sticker variant rotates -3 to -4°.
  • Aurora, two layers. Behind the home hero, HeroAurora drifts three large blurred orbs (violet, cyan, magenta) on coprime 19s / 23s / 27s loops. In front of those, .home-hero__aurora holds seven letter-orbs, one per letter of ZAYLIST, each coloured by that letter's place in the spectrum tape and started 1.4s earlier than the last so the glow travels across the word. Both sit over the hero background video.
  • Live dots: 8px neon dots with a 10px glow blink at 1.8s next to LIVE / open-to-work / kicker labels.

Tags & badges

  • Sticker tag (the filled standard): display font, black text, lit neon fill (default magenta), black keyline, top bevel and an accent floor bloom (--sticker-lit).
  • Event type tag: monospace, lime fill, 1.5px border, keyline and a small bloom. Day tag: white pill, black text. Meta tag: outline.
  • Sold out: prominent red label on cards and modal. CLAIMABLE: tag on unclaimed events linking to the claim flow. Verified: accent-filled chip on profiles. Pronouns: cyan outline pill.

Motion

  • 0.15s snappy on hovers/presses; ~4s ease-in-out ambient pulses. Buttons lift up-left on hover and press down-right. Cards lift 2px.
  • Entrance system (utility pages): hero fades up 10px over 0.48s, the stats band follows at +0.07s, feeds fade in at 0.35s, compose panels 0.25s, modals 0.2s. A scroll-reveal utility fades sections in on first view.
  • Signature keyframes live in tokens/effects.css: pdxReveal, pdxPop, pdxBlink (site also uses a 1.8s blink for live dots), pdxSpotPulse, pdxPulse, pdxMarquee, plus the animated-seam trio pdxSeamFlow / pdxSeamGlint / pdxSeamGlow. Seven web add-ons layer on top (each additive, silenced by calm/reduced-motion): Seam Charge (pdxSeamFill/pdxSeamGlint/pdxSeamFlash), Split-Flap (per-cell rotateX, the glyph swaps at the halfway point while the cell is edge-on; used in the footer sign-off), Live Wave (pdxEqBounce), Count It Up (pdxNumPop/pdxFloatUp), RSVP Sparks (pdxSparkFly), Ring Shimmer (the live client runs pdxaShimmer; pdxRingShimmer exists in effects.css but nothing references it) and the logo glitch. Beyond these, a Motion library card documents fifteen ambient and surface motions ported from the live client: the two aurora layers (three atmosphere orbs behind the hero, seven letter orbs in front of them), spectrum wave, rainbow divider flow, word glitch, sticker float, board flicker, schedule card entrance, attendance pop, sheet up, inbox overlay, flyer stash holo, the hero parallax, the hero overlay videos and the generic --parallax-y containers. Every entry there copies a rules block. See the Motion and Motion library cards.
  • Calm mode ([data-calm] / .calm-mode) and reduced motion silence grain, glitch, aurora, ticker, glows, entrance animation, and the home hero background video (poster still shown instead); seams go static; day colors flatten to grey; flag rings stay (they are meaning, not decoration).

ICONOGRAPHY

  • The brand ships a 32-icon line set (assets/icons/, 24px grid, 2.2 stroke, round caps, spectrum-colored) covering community, events, boards, safety, and nav. In components, icons are inline SVGs so they inherit color with no dependency. There is no brand icon font.
  • Emoji are not used as UI icons (the "✦" sparkle and the chain ring's padlock charm aside). Meaning is color-coded by day/admission/category, not iconified.
  • The brand mark is the wordmark lockup and the Z. Nav and footer use the full baked ZAYLIST wordmark assets/logo-lockup.png (Logo variant="full"); the square Z in assets/logo-z.jpg covers watermarks and in-product placements. Home-screen icons and favicons are a separate system: app-face/ holds the App face standards and every shipped export (180 iOS, 192 and 512 Android, maskable, monochrome, 1024 master). That package is the only source of truth for the app face, so nothing else here restates its artwork or sizes.

AVATARS & FLAG RINGS (canonical)

One component, three layers: a conic-gradient flag ring (4px at 48px, scales via --avatar-size), a cover-fit photo (user-cropped) or an initial fallback on one of six neon chips, and an optional charm. 18 rings (rainbow, progress, lesbian, gay-men, bisexual, transgender, nonbinary, pansexual, genderfluid, genderqueer, intersex, asexual, aromantic, agender, leather, bear, chain + none); Progress flag is the default for unset values; chain is metallic with a padlock charm. The ring is identity, not status: never repurpose ring colors for roles (the HOST chip is colored by the event's day). Masked/anonymous = no ring. Calm mode keeps rings. Clusters overlap by ~-8px with a 2px #0b0b0b border + "12 going" count. A light-only shimmer may orbit the ring (Ring Shimmer).

CAVEATS

  • Fonts: no brand font files were provided. Barlow Condensed and Inter load from Google Fonts to match --font-display / --font-body; monospace uses the system ui-monospace stack, same as production. If real files exist, drop them in assets/fonts/ and swap the rules in tokens/fonts.css.
  • Hero overlay videos: the site's webm overlay files (/overlays/*.webm) are not bundled here; approximate with the CSS grain + scrim recipe when mocking heroes.
  • Map tiles: MapPanel renders the branded overlay (glowing day pins, legend, seams) over a dark faux-street background. Production uses Leaflet + CARTO Dark Matter tiles (already dark, no filter).
  • Event, place, and community copy in the UI kit is a representative subset of the real data, not the full live dataset.

KNOWN CODE GAPS (on master, flagged for fixing, not yet true in code)

  • The live footer sign-off and tagline currently contain em dashes ("no corporate parent: just someone…", "Made by Tucker: for the community…") in client/src/components/Footer.tsx, which breaks the project's own "no em dashes, ever" rule. They should be swapped to periods/commas, as written in CONTENT FUNDAMENTALS above.
