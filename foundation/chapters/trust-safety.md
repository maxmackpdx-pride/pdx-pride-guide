Trust is a condition of participation, not a feature attached to one board. Zaylist must make identity and account state understandable, make member controls effective, preserve consent and privacy, support accessible interaction, and show reliable state when people post, contact, report, block, meet, exchange, work, or reveal identity.

## Governing decision

Trust & Safety appears at the top of the Foundation Library. One human-first, agent-readable standard defines platform invariants, contextual guidance, accessibility expectations, measurement, severity, and escalation. A recurring read-only Trust & Safety Watcher keeps evidence and unresolved risk visible while Tucker retains policy, moderation, product, and publishing authority.

## Consistency lock

- Human safety and trust stay the baseline for every product and automation choice.
- Agent access must be safe, bounded, and documented; it must never weaken privacy or controls.
- Track and support agent use while keeping human escalation and consent as priority.
- No moderation, safety policy, or production behavior may change without Tucker approval.

## Member control invariant

A block is a real backend-enforced safety action. One member initiates it and the platform creates a two-way interaction barrier. While active:

- Neither person can contact the other through profiles, conversations, or board contact paths.
- Their direct conversations and unread counts are hidden.
- Mutual follows are removed.
- The blocked member is not notified.
- Unblocking restores the ability to interact but never restores follows automatically.

Report, Block, Safety, and Rules remain discoverable near the interactions where harm or uncertainty can occur. Explanatory copy never substitutes for a control the interface claims exists.

## Contextual safety

- Profiles explain public visibility without implying an obligation to reply.
- Conversations warn about personal details, suspicious links, payment pressure, off-platform moves, meeting, and withdrawal of consent.
- GifZ keeps exchanges free, minimizes exact-location exposure, and supports safer handoffs.
- Gigz makes duties, compensation, timing, discrimination, impersonation, and suspicious links explicit.
- Mizzed Connection remains private by default and requires each person to choose identity reveal.
- THE HAÜZ minimizes exact-address and financial exposure, explains verification and scam risks, and keeps first contact consent-based.

## Accessibility is part of safety

The watch includes keyboard focus, touch targets, contrast, readable type and metadata, reduced motion and Calm mode, glow restraint, overflow, clipped content, loading/error/empty states, and unambiguous identity, account, moderation, and completion states. Safety-critical meaning never depends on color alone.

## Evidence and privacy

The seven-day product-experience view includes median time-to-content with sample count; posting and contact attempts and completions; report-control views, opens, and completed reports; and counter-mismatch occurrences.

Product analytics store only allowlisted event names, constrained surfaces, optional numeric values, anonymous visitor/session IDs, and authenticated user IDs already available to the platform. They do not store post text, message bodies, report reasons, usernames, precise locations, credentials, or inferred sensitive identity.

## Public automation policy

Automated readers, scrapers, and helper agents may be welcomed only as a secondary lane on public pages.

- Discovery remains open, friendly, and useful for humans.
- Any machine-readable extraction, protected content feed, or privileged tool route must require sign in and explicit authorization.
- Public pages should include a clear return path into human support, signup, and account help so people or agents can be redirected to verified flows.
- No internal states, private controls, or moderation actions should be exposed to unauthenticated automation traffic.

## Watcher boundary

The Trust & Safety Watcher may observe approved evidence, compare expected behavior with verified production, preserve a minimal snapshot, classify severity, append an internal source-linked note, and recommend the smallest safe next action.

It may not moderate an account or post, change a block, contact a member, read private content beyond explicitly authorized minimum evidence, publish or revise policy, spend money, change production, or promote its own recommendation into accepted Foundation or Design System law.

Critical findings include block bypass, privacy exposure, unauthorized access, active exploitation, or credible immediate physical danger and are routed to Tucker immediately. High findings receive same-day review. Medium findings appear in the weekly brief. Low findings remain in the tracked backlog.
