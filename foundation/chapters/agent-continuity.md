# Agent Continuity Ledger

Status: Current Foundation domain  
Owner: Tucker_PDmaX  
Operational records: `foundation/agent-continuity/`

Zaylist agents need continuity across chats without turning conversation fragments into product truth. The Agent Continuity Ledger preserves concise, attributable working context so a later chat can understand what changed, what remains unresolved, and which source should be consulted next.

The ledger is an operational memory layer. It is not a second Foundation, a substitute for the canonical Design System, or permission for an agent to approve its own recommendation.

## Human owner, agent helpers

The platform is built for humans first.  
Agents are support systems, not the primary decision makers for user experience, policy, or publishing.

## Consistency lock

- Use human-first UI and policy as the baseline assumption.
- Build agent tools as helpers for documented, authorized workflows.
- Do not promote recommendations into Foundation, production standards, or policy without Tucker approval.
- Keep continuity for acceleration only; never use it as permission to overstep approvals.

This means:

- UX and copy changes stay oriented to real people.
- Agent readability and operational tooling should be built in as helpers, not as the default audience.
- Public agent-facing tools should require authorization and should never weaken member privacy or safety.

## Always-on skills baseline for continuity work

For continuity and handoff tasks, every replacement model should load these baseline skills:

- `zaylist-foundation`
- `zaylist-design-system`
- `zay-logo-design`
- `zaylist-living-guide-builder`

Use them as a shared execution contract. Read the relevant `SKILL.md` files, then follow the continuity files under `foundation/agent-continuity/` before making any role action.

## What belongs here

- A short summary of completed work and verified evidence.
- Tucker's explicit direction, quoted or faithfully summarized.
- Decisions that still need Tucker's confirmation.
- Open questions, blockers, and the next useful action.
- References to relevant conversations, reports, campaigns, decisions, and production evidence.
- Internal coordination notes between the Founder Advisor, Marketing Agent, Design Drift Auditor, and future bounded specialists.

## What does not belong here

- Full chat transcripts.
- Secrets, credentials, authentication material, or unnecessary personal data.
- Private founder context in a generally shareable note.
- Duplicated Design System rules or copied Foundation chapters.
- Unverified inference presented as fact.
- An agent's recommendation presented as approved direction.

## Status and confidentiality

Every note labels each important statement as one of: `founder-direction`, `accepted`, `verified-production`, `recommendation`, `draft`, `unknown`, `blocked`, `superseded`, or `internal-context`.

Every note also declares a visibility class:

- `internal-shareable`: authorized Zaylist agents may read and reference it.
- `internal-nda-protected`: authorized agents may read it only inside the private Zaylist workspace; it must not enter public materials or external model inputs.
- `public-approved`: Tucker has explicitly approved the material for public use.

Silence never means public approval.

## Promotion rule

Agents may append continuity notes. They may not promote those notes into the Foundation, Design System, production standards, public marketing claims, or durable decisions. Promotion requires Tucker's explicit approval and, when durable, a structured decision record.

## Current Founder Priority

Use the ledger to maintain a reliable handoff between the Founder Advisor, Marketing Agent, Design Drift Auditor, and future growth, analytics, conversion, partnerships, or product specialists while keeping confidential context and canonical authority clearly separated.
