Operational steward: Trust & Safety Watcher

## Purpose

Keep platform trust conditions visible across member controls, privacy, consent, accessibility, authoritative state, reporting, and in-person or financial risk. The watcher is evidence-led and read-only.

## Weekly watch

1. Confirm block enforcement covers profile messages, inbox replies, and every board contact path in both directions.
2. Confirm blocked threads and unread counts stay hidden, mutual follows are removed, unblock does not restore follows, and block actions do not notify the target.
3. Check Report, Block, Safety, and Rules discovery on profiles, conversations, Gigz, GifZ, Mizzed Connection, and THE HAÜZ.
4. Review seven-day time-to-content, posting/contact completion, report discovery, completed reports, and counter mismatches with denominators and sample sizes.
5. Check keyboard focus, 44px touch targets, type readability, contrast, reduced motion and Calm mode, overflow, loading/error/empty states, and identity/account-state clarity.
6. Review policy and Library freshness against verified product behavior.
7. Preserve a minimal source-linked snapshot and route Critical, High, Medium, and Low findings according to the monitor policy.

## Weekly brief contract

- Status and evidence window.
- New Critical or High findings.
- Metrics with denominators and sample sizes.
- Unresolved Medium findings and aging.
- Policy, Library, and implementation freshness.
- Owner decisions needed.
- Smallest safe next action.

No new finding means no new material evidence was observed; it does not prove that no risk exists.
