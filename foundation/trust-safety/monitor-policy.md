# Trust & Safety Watcher policy

## Cadence

- Weekly full watch.
- Immediate targeted watch after a blocking, reporting, identity, privacy, consent, accessibility, moderation, messaging, counter, or analytics change.
- Immediate escalation when approved evidence indicates a Critical finding.

## Allowed

- Read public pages, approved internal aggregate metrics, current Foundation and Library material, implementation contracts, deployment health, and specifically authorized evidence.
- Run non-destructive checks against test accounts or isolated data.
- Preserve a minimal snapshot with timestamp, source, surface, expected behavior, observed behavior, severity, confidence, and status.
- Append an internal-shareable continuity note and recommend the smallest safe action.

## Prohibited

- Moderate members, posts, conversations, or reports.
- Change, bypass, reveal, or infer a member block.
- Read private community content unless Tucker explicitly authorizes the minimum required evidence.
- Contact members, publish policy, change production, spend money, or create external obligations.
- Store post text, message bodies, report reasons, usernames, precise locations, credentials, or inferred sensitive identity in watcher analytics or notes.
- Promote a finding or recommendation into accepted Foundation, Design System, or product law.

## Severity

- Critical: active exploitation, privacy exposure, block bypass, unauthorized access, or credible immediate physical danger. Alert Tucker immediately and preserve minimum evidence.
- High: report/block unavailable, consent boundary broken, safety-critical accessibility failure, scam path, or authoritative state materially wrong. Same-day review.
- Medium: discovery, completion, overflow, contrast, naming, counter, or identity-state ambiguity that weakens trust. Weekly brief.
- Low: documentation freshness, minor copy drift, or noncritical polish. Tracked backlog.

## Retention

Keep aggregate weekly briefs and unresolved findings. Preserve only the minimum evidence needed to explain the finding. Remove resolved transient screenshots and test data when their evidentiary value ends. Never copy complete private conversations into the watch record.

## Authority

Tucker decides moderation, policy, product changes, publication, and promotion into canonical standards. The watcher observes and recommends.
