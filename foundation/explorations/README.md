# Explorations

Raw thinking, preserved uncondensed.

## Why this exists

Long working sessions produce more good material than they produce decisions.
Most of it is never wrong, just unresolved. Under normal summarization that
material is the first thing lost: a session gets condensed to its conclusions,
the conclusions get condensed to a rule, and the reasoning, the discarded
alternatives, and the ideas that were merely early all disappear.

This folder is the one place in the Foundation where **condensation is
prohibited**. An exploration entry is written once, in the founder's framing,
and is never rewritten to be shorter or cleaner.

## Authority

Explorations sit at the bottom of the authority order, alongside research,
prototypes, and open questions. They rank below Foundation recommendations.

An exploration is **not** founder direction, **not** an accepted decision, and
**not** a recommendation, even when it records something the founder said
enthusiastically. Ideas leave this folder only by being promoted into a
structured decision record, and promotion is an explicit act.

Agents must never cite an exploration as a rule, a standard, or a plan of
record. When an agent finds only an exploration on a topic, the correct answer
is that no decision exists yet.

## Rules

1. Append only. Entries are never edited for brevity, tone, or tidiness.
   Corrections are added as new dated notes inside the entry.
2. Preserve the founder's own words for the core idea. Paraphrase only for
   surrounding context, and mark it as such.
3. Record rejected and abandoned ideas with the reason. The reason is usually
   the most valuable line in the entry.
4. Record ideas that were merely early. `not-yet` is a real status and is not a
   synonym for `rejected`.
5. Give every idea a stable local id so a decision record can cite its origin.
6. Never invent resolution. `unresolved` stays `unresolved`.
7. An entry is closed when the session ends. Later thinking becomes a new entry
   that links back.

## Idea status values

- `unresolved` — raised, not concluded. The default.
- `not-yet` — good, deliberately deferred. Not rejected.
- `promoted` — became a decision record. Cite the record id.
- `rejected` — decided against. Record the reason.
- `superseded` — replaced by later thinking. Cite the successor.

## Entry format

Front matter, then one section per idea thread. See
`2026-08-06-z-namespace-and-product-shape.md` for a worked example.

## Relationship to agent-continuity

`agent-continuity/notes` records what changed and what was decided, for handoff.
Explorations record what was considered, including what was not decided. A
handoff note answers "where are we." An exploration answers "what did we already
think about, and why did we not do it."
