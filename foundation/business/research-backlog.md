# Business research backlog

The Business Planning Agent should scan only when the result could change a decision, assumption, risk, milestone, or plan section.

## Highest priority

- Verified-download implementation and analytics source of truth.
- Current production feature and conversion status.
- Portland audience and adoption evidence.
- Portland-first LGBTQ news and primary-source intelligence that can materially change a business risk, opportunity, cost, milestone, community-trust boundary, or next action.
- Broader LGBTQ industry, platform, advertising, privacy, payments, event, travel, and regulatory changes only when they create a plausible Portland consequence.
- Founder-finance decision ranges.
- Railway invoices/usage, GoDaddy products/renewals, and the minimum-necessary ChatGPT Finance Liaison handoff.
- Oregon LLC privacy, tax, registration, insurance, and local licensing questions.
- Comparable local discovery/community product business models.
- Trust-aligned sponsor and partner demand.
- Relevant grants, competitions, incubators, and non-dilutive programs with current deadlines.

## Later

- Seattle and San Francisco expansion evidence.
- Regulated investment paths.
- Hiring and employment structure.
- Multi-member ownership or corporate conversion.

## Evidence quality

Prefer official sources, first-party product evidence, contracts, financial records, verified analytics, and direct user/customer research. News, trend reports, estimates, and agent simulations are supporting evidence only and must be labeled.

Do not scan merely to accumulate links. Every retained source must identify what claim it supports, its limitations, when it was checked, and when it should be refreshed.

For news intelligence, also record the event date separately from the publication date, geography, affected Zaylist object or plan section, urgency, and the smallest safe response. Limit the weekly report to five material developments. Escalate credible immediate safety, legal, platform-access, funding, or service-continuity threats instead of waiting for the scheduled report.
