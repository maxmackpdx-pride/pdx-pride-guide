# Funding readiness and options

Status: Internal planning  
Fundraising authorized: No  
Investor solicitation authorized: No

## The question before the money

What exact milestone becomes achievable with outside money that cannot be reached safely through focus, revenue, partnerships, or non-dilutive support?

## Capital paths

| Path | What it is | Best use | Primary caution |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | Money earned from customers, sponsors, or partners | Validate willingness to pay | Must preserve trust and avoid misleading ads or claims |
| Grant/prize | Non-dilutive award under program terms | Specific public-interest or innovation work | Restricted uses, reporting, eligibility, uncertain timing |
| Sponsorship | Paid association or campaign support | Distribution and operating costs | Editorial independence, disclosure, community fit |
| Loan/credit | Repayable capital | Predictable short-term need with repayment source | Personal guarantees, interest, cash-flow risk |
| Friends/family | Gift, loan, or investment depending on terms | Small early support | Relationship risk; securities and documentation issues still apply |
| Crowdfunding rewards | Presales or rewards | Validate demand for a defined deliverable | Fulfillment obligations, platform fees, tax treatment |
| Regulation Crowdfunding | Securities sold through a registered intermediary | Broad regulated investment raise | Disclosure, financial statements, legal/accounting cost, ongoing reporting |
| Private placement | Securities offered under an exemption | Accredited or relationship-based investors | Solicitation limits, filings, restricted securities, counsel required |
| Equity/SAFE/note | Ownership or future ownership rights | Larger growth capital | Dilution, control, valuation, securities compliance |

## Readiness evidence

- [ ] Verified download definition and instrumentation.
- [ ] 90-day start date and weekly reporting.
- [ ] Cohort activation and return behavior.
- [ ] Entity and ownership records.
- [ ] IP ownership and contributor agreements.
- [ ] Historical P&L and cash-flow record.
- [ ] Founder contribution ledger.
- [ ] Use-of-funds budget with milestones.
- [ ] 12-month base, conservative, and no-raise plans.
- [ ] Risk register and privacy/safety controls.
- [ ] Investor narrative separated from public marketing claims.
- [ ] Qualified securities counsel identified before any offer.

## Current conclusion

Funding may be possible, but possibility is not readiness. The current sequence is to define the measurement contract, establish the business and finance baseline, prove adoption, and then choose the least costly capital path that fits the milestone.
