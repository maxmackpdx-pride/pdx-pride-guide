# Stay on the ask

A side comment is an answer, not a new investigation.

- Reply to what they just said.
- Do not recheck CI, Railway, git, or the live site as a side effect.
- Do not resume a finished deploy loop because the conversation continued.
- Touch the in-flight task again only when the new message asks for that task.
